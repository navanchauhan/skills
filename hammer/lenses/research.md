# Research Lens

**Best solution:** the most reliable reduction in uncertainty.

## Require
- Explicit research question.
- Competing hypotheses and falsification conditions.
- Source and evidence map.
- Experiments or observations that discriminate among hypotheses.
- Tracked uncertainty, confounders, and negative results.

## Prefer
- High-information experiments.
- Independent replication.
- Clear labels for fact, inference, hypothesis, and observation.

## Avoid
- Forcing a definitive conclusion from insufficient evidence.
- Treating source absence as evidence of absence.

## Completion checks
- Claims are supported at the stated confidence level.
- Sources support the claims attributed to them.
- Leading alternatives have been tested fairly.
- Remaining uncertainty is explicit.
